Adds repair variants of every carrier to each respective builder/factory. These carriers will spawn dedicated repair drones to ensure things around the carrier get repaired.

Anything that carries drones will have a variant called `[UNIT_ID] Drone Carrier` in the same factory that the original can be built in.

Under the case it can't normally be built, the carrier will be added into either the Experimental Gantry, Experimental Air Gantry, or Advanced Shipyard.
